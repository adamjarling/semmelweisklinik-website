bio:
  en: |
    Clown, visual artist, graphic designer, writer, and shiatsu practitioner.

    She trained at the University of Vienna and the Academy of Fine Arts Vienna, and has studied clowning, theatre, bodywork, and performance since 2013. She is part of VolxTheaterKarawane, the club culture collective Flamingo Club, and the performance collective id:dev. She co-founded the open-space gallery Wattestäbchen, has organized and hosted the open stage Salon Sardine since 2017, and has been part of the Semmelweisklinik collective since 2024. She lives and works in Vienna.
